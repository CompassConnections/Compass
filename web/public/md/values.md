# Our values

Compass is a free, public directory for finding your people — friends, partners or collaborators —
**based on who they are, not how they look**. Every feature we build and every rule we enforce comes
from a handful of values. This page puts them in one place, so you know what to expect from us and
what we all expect from each other.

## Depth over volume

Our only mission is to create more genuine human connections, and every decision has to serve that
goal. Compass is for people who want more than small talk: who write down what they value and what
they need from a partner or a friend, who read a profile before writing to the person behind it, and
who would rather have one real conversation than twenty openers.

That is why a profile takes a while to write, why you need a profile of your own before you can write
to anyone, and why a first message has to engage with what the other person wrote. If you want
volume, there are faster apps. We would rather be slow and real.

## Who you are, not how you look

Values, ideas and personality come first; photos are secondary. You can search people by the things
that actually predict whether you will get along — what they care about, what they believe, what they
want from a relationship, the niche interest nobody else shares — and the compatibility score is plain
arithmetic on answers people gave about themselves.

A first message is about what someone wrote, never about their appearance.

## You decide, not an algorithm

There is no hidden ranking and no black box deciding who you get to see. Every profile is in an open
directory that you can search and filter yourself, and you can save a search to be told when someone
new fits it, instead of scrolling. Whether a conversation is for friendship, romance or collaboration
is stated up front, and respecting what someone said they are and are not looking for is part of
reading their profile.

## Free, for everyone, forever

There is nothing to buy. No subscriptions, no paid tiers, no advertising, and no selling of your data
or your attention. Every feature is available to every member.

Compass runs on [donations](/support) and volunteer work. A donation buys no advantage whatsoever —
not visibility, not features, not moderation outcomes — and where every euro goes is published in the
[financials](/financials).

## Open and transparent

The code is [open source](https://github.com/CompassConnections/Compass), the budget is public, and
votes and decisions are on the record. We would rather show you how something works than ask you to
trust it. Your profile is yours too: you choose whether it is public or visible to members only, and
you can switch at any time.

When we tell you what Compass does, we try to say exactly what it does — no more. If a page here does
not match what the app does, that is a bug, and we want to [hear about it](/contact).

## Owned by its community

Compass is meant to be for human connection what Linux, Wikipedia and Firefox are for software and
knowledge: a public good, built by the people who use it, for the benefit of everyone.

It is governed democratically under a public [constitution](/constitution), with safeguards against
capture by any company or individual. Major decisions — scope, funding, rules — are [voted](/vote) on
by contributors, and introducing ads, paid features or data monetisation would take a community vote.

## Care and safety

Treat people as people. An open directory works both ways, so we build for the many genuine members and
make life hard for the few who are not: we review every report, act on patterns across accounts, and
publish a plain [safety guide](/safety) on spotting scams and meeting safely.

We are honest about the limits too. We do not run background checks or verify identities, and an open
directory cannot promise that everyone in it is safe. It can give you the tools and the information to
decide for yourself.

## Fair, and open to correction

Most of our safeguards are automatic, and automatic systems make mistakes. A hold is a precaution, not
a verdict: a human reviews every one, usually within 24 hours, and restores accounts that look genuine.
Nothing you wrote is lost in the meantime.

First messages are now checked before they are delivered, partly by an AI model that reads the message
next to both profiles. This is new, we are still tuning it, and like everything at the start it is not
perfect. If you get a refusal that you can edit and resend, do; if a decision seems unfair or out of
line with the values on this page, [tell us](/contact). That feedback is how we tune it.

## Rules we strongly enforce

Values are only as real as what happens when they are broken. These rules are enforced, by the app and by
moderators, and breaking them has consequences.

**Writing to someone**

1. **Write your own profile first.** You need a bio and some compatibility answers of your own before
   you can start a conversation, so the other person has something to go on too.
2. **React to what they wrote.** Your first message must pick up something specific from the profile: a
   value, a vision for their relationship, a prompt answer, a cause. First messages that could have been
   sent to anyone are sent back to be rewritten.
3. **Be curious about what they value.** Ask what matters to them, what they believe and what they want
   from a relationship, not only what they do on weekends.
4. **Never send the same opener twice.** A first message that matches one you already sent to someone
   else puts your account on hold for review.
5. **Don’t open on looks.** Comments on someone’s appearance get reported, and openers about looks put
   the account on hold.
6. **Respect what they are looking for.** Don’t propose a date to someone looking for friends only, and
   stop when someone has disengaged.

**Everywhere on Compass**

- **No scams, spam or solicitation.** No asking for money, crypto, gifts or investments, no advertising
  or recruiting, and no bulk messaging. Starting too many new conversations in a short time puts the
  account on hold automatically. Confirmed scams and spam are banned permanently.
- **No harassment, hate speech or threats**, and no repeated contact after someone has disengaged.
- **No nudity or sexually explicit content**, and no sexual pressure.
- **Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.** Suspected cases end in immediate removal
  and may be reported to law enforcement. Compass is for adults only.
- **Be who you say you are.** No fake profiles, other people’s photos, or one person running many
  accounts.
- **Don’t post other people’s information** — private details, photos or conversations, including
  screenshots of Compass conversations — without their consent.

The full wording is in the [terms](/terms). To report someone, use the ⋮ menu on their profile or in the
conversation; the person never learns who reported them. Where a decision is a judgement call rather than
a clear violation, you can contest it by [writing to us](/contact).
