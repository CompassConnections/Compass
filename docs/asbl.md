# Setting up a Belgian non-profit (ASBL)

A step-by-step checklist for registering Compass as an ASBL (association sans but lucratif) with its
registered office in Wallonia. Written in October 2026 from public guides (see [Sources](#sources)). It is not
legal advice. Check fees and rules against the official pages before you pay or sign anything.

## The legal form

An ASBL is governed by the Code des sociétés et des associations (CSA) of 2019. Because the registered office
is in Wallonia, the statutes, the filings and all official documents must be **in French**.

## Before you start

- [ ] **At least one co-founder.** An ASBL needs **two or more founders**, who can be people or legal entities.
      You plus one trusted person (a friend or relative) is enough. **No notary is needed** for a standard
      ASBL.
- [ ] **A registered office (siège) in French-speaking Wallonia.** If you live abroad, use a relative's address
      or a paid domiciliation service.
- [ ] **A board (organe d'administration) that meets the CSA minimum.** The board normally needs **at least 3
      directors**. If the association has fewer than 3 members, a board of 2 is allowed. Confirm this against
      the current text before you draft.
- [ ] **A disinterested purpose (but désintéressé) and activities (objet).** Write them broadly enough to cover
      how the project might grow, because changing them later means a paid statute amendment. A starting
      point for Compass:

  > L'association a pour but désintéressé de favoriser des liens sociaux authentiques et de lutter contre
  > l'isolement, notamment par le développement et l'exploitation d'outils numériques libres et gratuits, ainsi
  > que par toute activité contribuant directement ou indirectement à la réalisation de ce but.

  (Promoting meaningful social connection and reducing isolation, notably by developing and operating free,
  open-source digital tools, and by any activity that directly or indirectly contributes to this purpose.)

- [ ] **VAT (TVA).** Check whether you need a VAT registration if the association will sell anything, such as
      paid features or merchandise. Donations alone usually don't require one.

## Creating the ASBL

1. **Draft the founding deed (acte constitutif) and statutes (statuts)** in French. They must state:
   - the name, followed by "ASBL"
   - that the registered office is in the Walloon Region
   - the purpose and the activities
   - membership rules: admission, resignation and exclusion
   - how the board works: appointment, powers and representation
   - what happens to the assets if the ASBL is dissolved (they must go to a disinterested purpose)

   Free French model statutes are available from Captain.legal and monasbl.be.

2. **Sign the deed.** Every founder signs.

3. **File with the greffe du tribunal de l'entreprise** for the area of your registered office.
   - **Online (recommended).** Log in to **eGreffe** with your eID and a card reader, fill in the form, upload
     the deed and pay electronically. This works from abroad and is usually cheaper.
   - **On paper.** Send Formulaire I and Formulaire II (for the BCE register), the deed, and proof of payment.

4. **Receive your enterprise number (numéro d'entreprise).** The greffe registers the ASBL in the BCE (Banque-
   Carrefour des Entreprises), and an extract is published in the annexes of the **Moniteur belge** a few days
   later.

5. **Open a bank account** in the ASBL's name, using the enterprise number and the publication.

6. **Register the beneficial owners in the registre UBO** through MyMinfin. For an ASBL, these are the
   directors, the people authorised to represent it, and anyone else with control. **This must be confirmed
   every year, even if nothing has changed.** Founders often miss this step, and missing it can lead to fines.

7. **Start the members register (registre des membres).** A spreadsheet is fine. Keep it at the registered
   office. It is **not** filed with the greffe.

## Cost

Most of the cost is publication fees. As a reference point, Xerius lists about **€279** for publication in the
Moniteur belge and **€177** for the greffe. Check the current rates on the ejustice "frais de publication"
page before paying. Filing online is generally cheaper than filing on paper.

## Ongoing obligations

| What                                                                             | When             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Keep accounts (simplified accounts are allowed for small ASBLs)                  | Continuously     |
| Hold the assemblée générale and approve the annual accounts                      | Yearly           |
| File the annual accounts with the greffe                                         | Yearly           |
| File the legal-entities tax return (impôt des personnes morales)                 | Yearly           |
| Confirm the registre UBO entries                                                 | Yearly           |
| Publish board changes, office moves and statute amendments in the Moniteur belge | When they happen |

A Belgian accountant or ASBL support service can handle all of this for a small yearly fee.
